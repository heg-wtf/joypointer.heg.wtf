How it works

  • Point

    Left stick or D-pad. Speed and dead zone are yours to tune.

  • Click and drag

    A left-clicks, B right-clicks. Hold to drag. Remap to any button.

  • Scroll

    Right stick, both axes, smoothed so slow reading stays steady.

  • Type

    Y opens the macOS on-screen keyboard. Registered games pause control automatically.

Default mapping

Sensible out of the box. Yours in a click.

JoyPointer reads the pad through Apple's GameController framework and posts real mouse events, so every app sees an ordinary mouse. It lives in the menu bar and steps aside the moment a game you registered comes to the front.

Requirements

What you need

  • macOS 13 Ventura or later on Apple Silicon or Intel.
  • A macOS-compatible controller, paired in System Settings → Bluetooth first. 8BitDo pads use their Apple or D-Input mode.
  • Accessibility permission. macOS asks once; JoyPointer needs it to move the pointer and post clicks.
  • A license key. JoyPointer is a paid app and controls the pointer only after activation.

FAQ

Where is my license key?

It is in the Lemon Squeezy receipt email sent right after purchase, together with the download link. You can also open the order from that email at any time. Paste the key into JoyPointer → License → Activate.

How many Macs can I use it on?

One license activates up to 3 Macs. To move to a new Mac, open JoyPointer on an old one and choose “Deactivate this Mac”, or write to us and we will free a slot.

How do updates work?

JoyPointer checks for updates itself and installs them with one click, using the Sparkle framework with signed, notarized builds. Updates are included in the lifetime license.

What does the app send over the network?

Only two things, only when you activate, validate, or deactivate a license: your license key and this Mac's name, sent to the Lemon Squeezy License API over HTTPS. Update checks fetch a small signed feed from this site. There is no account, no analytics, and no telemetry. This website has no analytics either. See the privacy policy.

Why not the Mac App Store?

JoyPointer toggles the macOS Keyboard Viewer through Accessibility APIs and scans Steam libraries for game auto-suspend. Neither is possible inside the App Store sandbox, so JoyPointer is sold here and notarized by Apple for Gatekeeper.

Which controllers work?

Anything macOS lists as a game controller with an extended gamepad profile: 8BitDo (Apple / D-Input mode), Nintendo Switch Pro Controller, PlayStation DualShock 4 and DualSense, Xbox Wireless controllers, and most Bluetooth pads that follow the same profile.
